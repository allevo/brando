# A house is covered by services its level does not require

## What the code does

`compute_from_scratch` (`sim-core/src/coverage.rs`) offers **every** house within range as a
candidate, whatever that house's level asks for; the level is never read there. `production` then
feeds every house that came out of it with a food provider attached.

So a house on the first rung — since phase 13 a hut, which requires water only — is assigned to a
farm. It eats `food_per_resident` per resident per tick, and it takes places the farm counts against
its capacity.

## Why it could be a problem

The same assignment does two jobs at once, and only one of them looks wrong.

It **feeds the hut**, which costs the farm stock and places it could have given to a house that
really requires food. That is the part that looks like a leak.

It is also the **only way the hut is ever promoted**. Satisfaction moves on the union of what every
level requires, precisely so that a rung introducing a new service can be reached, and the rung above
the hut asks for food. Stop assigning food to the hut and its food accumulator sits at zero for ever:
nothing rises past the first level again.

So "assign only what the level requires" is not a one-line fix. It is a change to how the ladder is
climbed — which is why it is a question and not a bug.

Today the number of huts is bounded by how many houses the player builds. Migration fills them, and
every one it fills becomes a real claim on a farm sized for the rungs above. That is why the slot
sits before phase 15.

## The answer

**Yes — by design.** The two jobs the question separated do not pull apart: refusing the food
assignment to a hut does not stop a leak, it strands the hut on the first level forever, because
satisfaction on a service is the only thing a level-up ever reads, and it can only be earned by
receiving the service.

A farm's capacity is not sized for the houses that currently require food — it is sized for the
houses the player wants to carry through the level that requires it. Feeding a hut before its own
level asks for food is the price of that growth, not a leak from it. `RULES.md`'s Food section
already states the rule in full, and two tests already pin both halves of it:
`a_service_the_house_does_not_require_is_left_alone` (`sim-core/tests/satisfaction.rs`) — a service
outside a house's own level still covers and feeds it, and its accumulator for that service stays
still regardless — and `a_level_that_asks_for_more_is_not_reached_on_the_service_below`
(`sim-core/tests/levels.rs`) — a house cannot rise into a level whose required service it has not
received, and does rise once it has. Both were written when the levels system was built; the test the
roadmap slot asks for was already in the tree.

**What this hands to phase 15.** Migration multiplies how many huts draw on a farm sized for the
levels above them — today the player controls the count by choosing when to build. That is a
capacity-planning problem for the player, not a correctness problem for the coverage rule, and phase
15 inherits it knowingly rather than by surprise.
